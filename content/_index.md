Olá! My name is Eduardo Adame, and I'm a PhD student in the [School of Mathematics at the University of Edinburgh](https://maths.ed.ac.uk/) and the [Maxwell Institute for Mathematical Sciences](http://maxwell.ac.uk/), supervised by [Jordan Richards](https://jbrich95.github.io/) and [Miguel de Carvalho](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~mdecarv/). My PhD is fully funded by a School of Mathematics studentship, and my research focuses on generative AI for statistics of extremes.

I hold a master's degree in Applied Mathematics from the [School of Applied Mathematics](https://emap.fgv.br/en) of the Getulio Vargas Foundation (FGV EMAp). There, supervised by [Luiz Max de Carvalho](https://github.com/maxbiostat) and co-supervised by [Flávio B. Gonçalves](https://est.ufmg.br/~fbgoncalves/), I developed exact computation methods for the Normalised Power Prior. I also contributed to work on uncertainty quantification for generative image restoration using conformal prediction, and worked on Gaussian processes for emulating functions.

Beyond research, I have worked on applied projects. From July 2023 to June 2025, I was a consultant at The World Bank, carrying out a geospatial counterfactual analysis of the impact of protected areas on carbon emissions in Latin America and the Caribbean. I have also worked with Brazil's Ministry of Finance, evaluating and statistically validating legal data systems.

Please check out my [CV](/cv/) for more information. If I can help you with anything, feel free to contact me.
